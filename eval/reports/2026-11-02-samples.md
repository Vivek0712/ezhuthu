# Try-it samples: masked VAL passages with a known edition reading (2026-11-02)

> **Why.** User feedback on PR #38: "almost every single sample ... says it needs expert opinion." All 13 previous
> try-it samples were genuine lacunae: letters the DHARMA editors themselves did not restore. A live run of those 13
> (24 gaps) abstained on 23, with top-candidate confidence 0.05 to 0.15, so the gallery only ever showed the
> abstain path. The new primary samples are **masked** passages: a span the editor did restore is hidden, the
> restorer proposes a reading, and the app then shows "The published edition reads: X" with the match state.
> "Matches the editors' restoration" means the fill equals the DHARMA editor's supplied text after
> `normalise_for_scoring`; it does not mean "correct".

## Selection rule (written and committed before any live run)

Code: `select_masked()` in `scripts/build_samples.py`.

1. **Pool:** every item of `benchmark/items.val.jsonl` (VAL split only; never TEST; never TRAIN, because the
   5-gram and retrieval read train text and would make results there leaky and too good) with a gap of 1 to 8
   Tamil letters (`gap_chars`), whose edition declares CC BY-SA 4.0 and whose dynasty is one of the app's five
   periods (Pallava, Pāṇḍya, Cōḻa, Rāṣṭrakūṭa, Vijayanagara). Hard and non-hard items alike (VAL is 90% hard); the
   `hard` flag is recorded per sample.
2. **Context:** the item's own `left` / `right` context, cut at the nearest other gap marker on each side so that
   the passage has exactly one gap; left keeps at most 160 characters and right at most 100, cut to whole words
   (a side cut at another gap also starts or ends on a whole word). Readable: at least 30 letters left and 15
   right, and the API's own input check accepts the passage in ISO and in Tamil script.
3. **Draw:** the pool sorted by item id, shuffled with `random.Random(38)`; then round-robin over the periods in the
   app's order (Pallava, Pāṇḍya, Chola, Rāṣṭrakūṭa, Vijayanagara), each period taking its next shuffled item from an
   inscription not yet used, until 10 items are picked or the pool runs out.
4. **Run:** every picked item is run once through `EzhuthuService.restore` (deployed configuration,
   `config/models.yaml`; `EZHUTHU_LIVE=1`, profile `heisenberg`) by `scripts/record_sample_restores.py`, with a
   hard cap of 40 Bedrock calls for the whole recording. Every result is reported as it comes out; no item is
   replaced, whatever the result.

**Unsolved group (kept from the old gallery):** three genuine lacunae, chosen for few gaps and period spread before
the run: `hero-stone-naiya-vatukan` (Pallava, 2 gaps), `mamallapuram-calf-pasture` (Pallava, 1 gap),
`tiruvannamalai-krishnadevaraya` (Vijayanagara, 1 gap). There is no edition reading to compare; the expected
behaviour is that the system proposes options and asks an expert.
