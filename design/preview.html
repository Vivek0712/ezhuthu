Brand & design system · v1

A letter that remembers where it came from.

Ezhuthu teaches learners to read 2,000 years of Tamil inscriptions, in Tamil. The system borrows its materials from the inscriptions themselves: stone, palm leaf, laterite, lamp-black ink, and the temple lamp that lets you read them.

கல், ஓலை, செங்கல் நிறக் கல், விளக்குக் கரி மை, கோயில் விளக்கு: கல்வெட்டுகளின் பொருள்களே இதன் வடிவமைப்பு.

01

Logo system

The mark is the modern Tamil letter எ (e) with its own ancestor, the Tamil-Brahmi e (a triangle), set inside its bowl. The Tamil wordmark carries the same letter. All logos are pure paths: no font is needed to display them.

03

Typography

Tamil leads. Noto Serif Tamil for display, Noto Sans Tamil for reading, Fraunces as the single Latin display face, Instrument Sans for UI. Era scripts use Noto Sans Brahmi and Noto Sans Grantha. All SIL Open Font License: see brand/FONTS.md.
