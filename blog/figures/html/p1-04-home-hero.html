A real Thanjavur stone, read letter by letter

One recorded animation of a Chola-period inscription, four steps: Reader, Restorer, confidence check, Interpreter.

1

The Reader names 46 carved letters, each with its own probability. Mean 0.65, self-reported, not calibrated.

2

The least certain letter, at 0.45, goes to the Restorer as a gap. Opus 4.6 proposes fills for it.

3

No fill clears the calibrated threshold, so it stays open. The "?" waits for an expert instead of a guess.

4

11 words, a phrase in today's Tamil and its meaning. Meaning by Ezhuthu; no published edition to compare against.
