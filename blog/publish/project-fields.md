Title (107 chars):
Ezhuthu: An Apprentice for Tamil Temple Inscriptions, Built with Strands Agents on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore

Description (294 chars):
Ezhuthu teaches learners to read real, damaged Tamil inscriptions in Tamil, and proposes the worn-away letters with a calibrated confidence, leaving the rest for an expert. On 413 held-out gaps it matches the editors' restoration 62.7% of the time, and 9 in 10 of the fills it commits to match.
