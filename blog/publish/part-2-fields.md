Inside Ezhuthu: Six Strands Agents, a Tamil Epigraphy Ontology over MCP and a 25-Second Budget on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore

Description (482 chars):
The agent pipeline behind Ezhuthu, an apprentice for Tamil temple inscriptions. A Strands Graph runs every lesson with a Tutor and Assessor Swarm inside it; a tool-using Restorer fuses parallel passages, a character 5-gram and Claude into one calibrated score; a custom ontology of 1,958 kings, places, temples and formulas reaches the agents over MCP; Sonnet 4.6 escalates to Opus 4.6 on Amazon Bedrock; and everything answers inside 25 seconds on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Runtime.
