## How the cut applies these (graphics pass, 2026-11-03)

- Every scene, including the stock-only ones, is a deterministic HTML page in `media/video/scenes/<id>.html`, rendered
  frame by frame by `media/video/render.mjs` to `ezhuthu-video/scenes/<id>.mp4`. The warm grade (sepia/contrast
  filter, amber soft-light wash, lifted blacks) and film grain (seeded per frame) are applied in the page
  (`scenes/_lib/kit.js`, `scene.css`), so the Commons originals in `ezhuthu-video/stock/` stay untouched.
- Photo plates are 4096 px downscales in `ezhuthu-video/stock/web/` (p17 at its full 6000 px); moving images are
  all-intra trims in `ezhuthu-video/clips/` made by `media/video/prep-clips.sh`.
- Each scene shows a small on-screen credit line for the photo or clip it uses; the end card (`c-22-close`) lists
  every asset above with title, author and licence.
- Live-site captures (`c-capture`) were re-recorded on 2026-11-03 for `restore-fill` (sample
  `tiruvalisvaram-district-name`: filled -k at 72%, matches the editors' restoration) and `practice` /
  `practice-mobile` (a correctly answered letter drill). The earlier `restore-fill` take (Tiruvāvaṭutuṟai) abstained on
  the day, so it could not illustrate a fill; it is kept as `footage/restore-fill-tiruvavaduturai-open.mp4`.

## Final assembly and derived outputs (compile.sh, 2026-11-03)

- `media/video/compile.sh` joins the 22 scene renders with dissolves/match cuts, mixes narration (Amazon Polly) over
  the original score and SFX, and writes the film, a burned-captions version, the thumbnail, two 15 s teasers and a
  contact sheet into `ezhuthu-video/` (never into git). No external asset is added at this stage beyond those listed
  above.
- **Thumbnail** (`ezhuthu-video/thumbnail.png`): p14, Brihadeeswarar temple evening, Thanjavur, Tamilnadu.jpg,
  Cafeskool, **CC BY-SA 4.0 (share-alike)**, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Brihadeeswarar_temple_evening,_Thanjavur,_Tamilnadu.jpg.
  Modifications: cropped to 16:9, light sepia/brightness grade, dark gradient, Ezhuthu lockup and text over it.
  The credit "Photo: Cafeskool, CC BY-SA 4.0 (cropped, graded)" is printed on the thumbnail; the thumbnail is shared
  under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **Teasers** (`teaser-15s.mp4`, `teaser-15s-vertical.mp4`): cut from the finished film (0.0-4.9 s p17 + p04 opening,
  47.1-53.7 s live hero with Richard Mortel's photo, CC BY 2.0, 172.1-176.2 s close over p05, Vyacheslav Argenberg,
  CC BY 4.0). The on-picture credit lines from the film stay visible; the vertical version blurs the same frames as
  its background. Because p17 (CC BY-SA 4.0) is included, the teasers are also CC BY-SA 4.0; put the full credit list
